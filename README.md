# Reading Exercise (working title)

Duolingo-style reading activity and skill-building game for kids aged 8–16, designed for underprivileged learners in Uganda and elsewhere. The app supports home learning and is intended to run offline on Android phones.

---

## Purpose

Many children in low-resource settings have weak reading skills and limited access to quality materials.  
This app aims to:

- Make reading **fun and game-like** (short activities, quick feedback).
- Work **fully offline** once installed.
- Support **local languages and English**.
- Fit naturally into the **Afterschool Geekery Uganda** ecosystem.

---

## Target Group

- Children and teens: **8–16 years**
- Underprivileged learners with limited access to books, internet, or tutoring
- Home learning on low-end Android phones (and later other platforms)

---

## Core Experience (planned)

Once the app is ready, a learner will:

1. **Choose profile & settings**
   - Age range  
   - Difficulty level  
   - Topic / theme (e.g. everyday life, school, animals, etc.)

2. **Get tailored reading exercises**
   - Short texts appropriate to age and level  
   - Interactive tasks to check understanding (questions, word ordering, etc.)

3. **Listen to each text**
   - **Luganda**: pre-recorded audio  
   - **English**: text-to-speech (TTS) or pre-recorded audio

4. **Earn rewards**
   - Permanent scoring / points
   - Streaks for daily practice
   - Simple achievement system to keep motivation high

---

## Role in the Bigger Project

This app is planned as the **first module** of a broader literacy learning platform:

- Multiple small “games” and activities under one roof  
- Shared user profile, progress, and rewards across games  
- Integration into the **Afterschool Geekery Uganda** website and Android app

Reading Exercise is the **first game** in that future suite.
